Individual Sessions

Athletes meet 1-on-1 with Sabrina in these 50-minute sessions to strengthen their mindset through a personalized plan tailored to their individual needs. During sessions, athletes learn specific mental skills and develop healthier, more productive ways of thinking to help them perform at their best.
50-minute sessions involve learning mental skills through personal reflection, activities, discussion, watching videos, and actually practicing mental skills in real time. There is no long-term commitment required.

Online Learning Modules

Online mental performance training for athletes. These short, self-paced modules offer a more flexible, schedule-friendly alternative to traditional 50-minute 1-on-1 sessions. Athletes learn practical mental skills to build confidence, bounce back from mistakes, and strengthen their mindset to perform at their best.
Every module is paired with a 30-minute, 1-on-1 session with Sabrina to help your athlete apply what they've learned to their specific sport and individual needs. Each module is personally created by Sabrina, using the same mental skills, strategies, and examples taught in her traditional 50-minute sessions. Sabrina also hand-picks each module based on your athlete's unique needs and goals, combining the convenience of self-paced learning with the benefits of personalized coaching.

Team Sessions

All sports! Mental performance training is beneficial for athletes across all sports, ages, and competitive levels.

Mental performance training isn't just for athletes who are struggling. It's for any athlete who wants to get the most out of their mindset and performance!

Mental performance training may be especially beneficial if your athlete:

  • Struggles with confidence or self-doubt
  • Has difficulty bouncing back after mistakes
  • Performs better in practice than in competition
  • Gets nervous or overwhelmed during their sport
  • Struggles with negative self-talk or overthinking
  • Has difficulty staying focused or motivated
  • Wants to become more consistent and mentally resilient

Even athletes who are already confident and performing well can benefit from developing stronger mental skills. Sabrina has helped many athletes enhance their mindset through trainings on leadership, communication, focus, goal setting, motivation, performing under pressure, and much more.

Confidentiality Statement

Confidentiality & Privacy

Sabrina has worked within the Atlanta volleyball community for more than seven years and understands the importance of maintaining complete professionalism and confidentiality. Throughout that time, as a volleyball coach and a performance consultant, she has maintained a strong record of protecting the privacy and confidentiality of her clients.

All client relationships are confidential. Confidentiality is a professional and ethical commitment outlined within the Association for Applied Sport Psychology’s ethical standards that Sabrina follows as a Certified Mental Performance Consultant® (CMPC). The Mental Gain does not disclose the identity of its clients or confirm whether an individual is currently or has previously worked with Sabrina. Client identity, working relationship, session discussions, performance concerns, progress, and other details related to services are kept private and are not shared with coaches, clubs, administrators, or other third parties. For clients under the age of 18, general information or progress may be shared with a parent or legal guardian.

The Mental Gain provides mental performance consulting, helping athletes, teams, coaches, and performers develop mental skills for performance optimization. Mental health treatment, counseling, and psychotherapy are outside the scope of services, and are not provided by The Mental Gain.
