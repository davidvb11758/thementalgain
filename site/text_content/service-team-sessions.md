## Description

**ID:** `service-team-sessions-p1`

Every team is different, which is why each session is customized to address your team's unique needs and goals. Sessions are interactive and engaging, combining hands-on activities, team discussions, personal reflection, and opportunities to practice mental skills together. Common topics include communication, resilience, performing under pressure, maximizing team performance, motivation, and more\!

**ID:** `service-team-sessions-p2`

**Sessions are available virtually or in person\!** Virtual sessions can be completed with your entire team together in one room, or with athletes joining individually from the comfort of their own home. In-person sessions are available throughout Georgia, and out-of-state travel is also an option\! With Sabrina’s access to discounted airfare, we can make traveling to your team more affordable. **No long-term commitment is required.**
