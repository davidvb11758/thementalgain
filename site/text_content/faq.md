**ID:** `faq-q2`

How do I know if mental performance training is right for my athlete?

**ID:** `faq-a2`

**Mental performance training isn't just for athletes who are struggling. It's for any athlete who wants to get the most out of their mindset and performance!**

Mental performance training may be especially beneficial if your athlete:

* Struggles with confidence or self-doubt
* Has difficulty bouncing back after mistakes
* Performs better in practice than in competition
* Gets nervous or overwhelmed during their sport
* Struggles with negative self-talk or overthinking
* Has difficulty staying focused or motivated
* Wants to become more consistent and mentally resilient

Even athletes who are already confident and performing well can benefit from developing stronger mental skills. Sabrina has helped many athletes enhance their mindset through trainings on leadership, communication, focus, goal setting, motivation, performing under pressure, and much more.

Do you offer virtual or in-person sessions?

**ID:** `faq-a3`

Both! The Mental Gain primarily operates virtually, but in-person sessions are also available.

**Individual Athletes**

* About 95% of individual clients meet virtually for easier scheduling and convenience.
* In-person sessions are available upon request, depending on location within Georgia and availability.

**Teams**

* Virtual sessions are available worldwide.
* In-person sessions are available throughout Georgia.
* Out-of-state teams: Sabrina can travel to you! Her personal access to discounted airfare helps make out-of-state sessions more accessible and affordable.

Do I have to commit to a certain number of sessions?

**ID:** `faq-a4`

Not at all! The Mental Gain doesn't require long-term contracts or commitments.

Sabrina believes it's important that both she and your athlete feel they're a good fit. Sessions are scheduled and paid for individually, giving you the flexibility to continue training at a pace that works for your athlete's needs, goals, and schedule.
